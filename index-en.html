#About me

For more than 30 years I've been working in what I love most: creating unforgettable experiences for people — first as a DJ in some of the most iconic clubs in Magaluf, and now in global tourism and theme parks operations.

Today I work as Theme Parks Executive – Global Operations at HBX Group (Hotelbeds), focused on theme parks & attractions for major global brands. I connect B2B partners, internal teams and final travellers to make sure every booking, ticket and incident is handled with agility, empathy and attention to detail.

My background combines:

  • Tourism & DMC operations (Mallorca incoming, Jet2, LMK, etc.)
  • Theme parks & attractions (major global parks)
  • Customer service & crisis management (complex bookings, last-minute issues, VIP needs)
  • Technology & tools (Salesforce, GCO, Excel, reporting, AI tools to improve productivity)

I'm bilingual Spanish/English, used to working with international teams and partners. Colleagues usually describe me as someone who takes ownership of problems until they are solved, brings calm, humour and perspective in stressful situations, and uses data and tools to improve processes — not only to follow procedures.

"People remember how you make them feel." My past as a professional DJ taught me that; today I apply the same mindset to tourism operations — combining structure and empathy to deliver reliable, memorable service.

I also care deeply about how service is experienced. Accessibility isn't a "nice-to-have" — it's a real test of customer experience and service standards, and it often fails quietly in the gaps between processes.
